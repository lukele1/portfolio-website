Practice sites

A different building every time you refresh (20 in all), or hit Shuffle to mix the numbers into a brand new site. Work it on paper the same way you would on site, type your answers, and check them. Round to one decimal; anything within about half a dB counts. All antenna gains are in dBd.

Made-up sites for practice. Same math as the BDA Setting Gain pages. Rules of thumb: no radio info = 33 dBm for 700/800, 35 dBm for VHF/UHF · dBd = dBi − 2.15 · dBm = 10·log(W × 1000) · ½" plenum ≈ 2.2 dB/100 ft at 700/800, 1.5 at UHF, 1.0 at VHF.
